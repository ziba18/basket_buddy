Description 1,066 / 4,000

Basket Buddy is the shopping list built for people who share a home. Add items to one shared list that updates instantly for everyone in your household — roommates, partners, family. No more group texts about who's picking up milk, no more duplicate trips to the store. WHAT IT DOES – One shared list, always in sync — add an item and everyone sees it appear in real time – Smart autocomplete suggests common items and their usual units as you type – Check items off as you shop, then optionally log who bought them, the price, and where – A dedicated Purchased tab keeps a running history of what your household has bought – Invite housemates with a simple 6-character code – Works instantly from cache, then syncs the moment you're back online – Sign in with Apple, Google, or email – Light and dark mode, matching your device Basket Buddy doesn't show ads, doesn't sell your data, and only shares your list with the people you invite into your home. Delete your account and your data anytime, right from Settings. One basket. One household. Always up to date.

Screenshot Shot List

Both stores

Six screens tell the whole story. Suggested order and the one line each should sell — use these as overlay captions if you're compositing marketing screenshots, or just as a shot list if submitting plain device captures.

  • 1. Welcome screen — "Sign in with Apple, Google, or email." Establishes the sign-in options up front.
  • 2. Shopping list, mid-use — "One shared list, always in sync." Show a realistic list with a few categories and at least one item another member added.
  • 3. Add-item autocomplete — "Smart suggestions as you type." Mid-keystroke on the add-item field with the suggestion list visible.
  • 4. Purchased tab with a logged purchase — "Track who bought what, and where." Show the purchase-log modal or a completed entry with price/store/date.
  • 5. Settings — invite code — "Invite your household in one code." The 6-character invite code prominent and legible.
  • 6. Dark mode variant of #2 — "Looks right, day or night." Same shopping-list screen, dark theme — cheap to capture, shows polish.
