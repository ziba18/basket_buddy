Basket Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Basket Buddy is a shared shopping-list app for households ("Homes"). This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can remove it. If anything here is unclear, contact zamaniziba18@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressAccount sign-in (email/password, Google, or Apple)
PasswordOnly if you sign up with email/password — stored as a salted hash, never in plain text, by our auth provider (Supabase Auth)
Name / nicknameShown to other members of your Home so you're identifiable in a shared list
Shopping list itemsThe core feature — items, categories, quantities you add
Purchase historyOptional — who bought an item, price, store, and date, if you choose to log it
Home membershipWhich household(s) you belong to, so the right people see the right list

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and the app has no advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.

Sign-in with Google or Apple

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and name from that provider to create your account — we never see your Google or Apple password.

Who can see your data

Your nickname, added items, and purchase logs are visible to other members of the same Home (that's the point of a shared list) — never to members of a different Home, and never publicly. Your email address is not shared with other members.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, which processes it on our behalf under its own security and privacy commitments. We don't sell or share your data with any other third party.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. This immediately and irreversibly removes your profile, email, and login credentials. Items you added or purchases you logged stay visible to your former housemates (with your name removed) so the household's shared list and history stay intact — the same way removing a roommate from a shared spreadsheet wouldn't blank out the rows they'd already filled in.

You can also leave a Home at any time from Settings without deleting your account.

Children's privacy

Basket Buddy is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
